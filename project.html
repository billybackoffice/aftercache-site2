Mortal Coil:
Autonoetic

The songs on this project were created anywhere from two to five years ago. Some were finished, some were never fully realized, and some are simply ideas that existed for a moment in time before life moved on.

I know these songs aren’t perfect. Some of them probably don’t sound the way I would make music today. But that’s exactly why they mean something to me. Each one represents a period, an idea, or a version of myself that played a role in shaping who I am now.

Think of this project as me clearing the room.

I'm putting these old songs out into the world so I can make space for everything that's coming next. They're pieces of an older version of me, and releasing them feels like closing a chapter without pretending it never happened.

This isn't necessarily where I'm going.
It's where I've been.
And now, I'm ready to make room for what's next.
